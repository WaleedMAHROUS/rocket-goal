How to play

Phone

  • Left thumb: touch anywhere on the left side and drag. Up drives, down reverses, sideways steers.
  • Boost and Jump sit side by side on the right. Rest your thumb between them to press both.
  • Jump: tap to jump. Tap again while pushing the stick to flip that way.
  • In the air the stick turns the car only after you move it, so holding forward to drive will not spin you.
  • Roll: hold on the ground to drift. Hold in the air and push the stick sideways to spin.

Keyboard and mouse

W A S D or arrowsDrive and steer. In the air: tilt and turn Left click / ShiftBoost Right click / SpaceJump. Press again with a direction to flip Ctrl / XDrift, and free air roll Q EAir roll left / right C EscBall cam, pause. Gamepads work too

Tricks to learn

  • Aerial: jump, tilt back, then boost toward the ball.
  • Flick: carry the ball on your roof, then jump and flip.
  • Pinch: smash the ball into the curved ramp or a wall and it fires out like a cannon.
  • Flip reset: touch the ball with all four wheels in the air to get your flip back.
